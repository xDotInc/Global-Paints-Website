About Global Paints

Built in Bulawayo, made for African conditions.

The story, vision, and mission behind Global Paints & Coatings.

Our story

A trusted local manufacturer, serving clients countrywide.

Global Paints & Coatings is a trusted paint manufacturer based in Bulawayo, Zimbabwe, serving clients countrywide. The company is reliable, eco-friendly, and value-driven, manufacturing paints tailored to customer needs and serving homeowners, businesses, and construction companies.

Founded by Milton Manda, Global Paints & Coatings has grown as heard on Skyz Metro FM 100.3, with an ambition to become Zimbabwe's number-one top-selling paint company.

As a fast and stable growing company based in Bulawayo, our aim is to supply every company, home, and customer a well-deserved paint that lasts for decades while maintaining its beauty and texture over time.

Our vision

To be a leading manufacturer and supplier

To be a leading manufacturer and supplier of high-quality paints and coating solutions in Zimbabwe and the region, recognised for excellence, innovation, reliability, and a commitment to enhancing and protecting the built environment.

Our mission

Performance and aesthetic excellence

Global Paints & Coatings is dedicated to producing and supplying paints that combine performance and aesthetic excellence. We are committed to superior product quality, professional services, and sustainable solutions for our customers, partners, and communities.
